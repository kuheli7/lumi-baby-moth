Character sheet
(draft)

1. NAME
Lumi
2. WHAT THEY ARE
A fuzzy baby moth the size of a marshmallow. A natural night owl: curious, easily distracted, and happy in seconds (especially with cookies). When its feelings get hurt, its wings droop. When they get really hurt, it flops over and plays dead.
3. WHAT THEY DO
Night-shift Hobby Hopper and Curator of Useless Facts. Lumi flies toward every glowing thing and picks up a new hobby each night:
  • 🎨 watercolor moons
  • ✨ resin "jar of stars" charms
  • 📿 bead jewellery made from dew drops
  • 🎸 one guitar string it found (still learning)
  • 🧠 a lamp-lit museum of useless facts
4. THEIR STORY
Lumi hatched inside a forgotten paint box under a desk lamp. That lamp was the first light it ever saw, and it has been chasing lights (and new hobbies) ever since. It has never finished a single one… except the little lantern it carries. In the final scene, that lantern lights up the whole site.
5. TAGLINE
"Ooh, shiny. New hobby."
