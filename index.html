About Lumi

the 5-point character sheet

1Name
Lumi
2What they are
A marshmallow-sized, fuzzy baby moth. A chaotic, curious night owl. A cookie makes it happy in seconds. Hurt feelings make it droop and cry, and if it gets worse it flops (plays dead, x_x) until someone feeds it.
3What they do
Night-shift Hobby Hopper and Curator of Useless Facts. Starts a new hobby every night. Finishes none.
4Their story
Hatched in a paint box under a desk lamp and has chased lights (and hobbies) ever since. The only thing it ever finished is the lantern it carries.
5Tagline
“Ooh, shiny. New hobby.”

              About Lumi

              Lumi is an original character by Lumi's human (a fellow night owl).

              Art

              Lumi's reference sketches were made with ChatGPT image generation, and Lumi was then drawn as SVG code with Claude. The attic and hobby icons are hand-made SVG; a few small icons are your device's emoji.

              Fonts

              Fredoka and Patrick Hand, from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

              Sounds

              Made live in your browser with the Web Audio API. No audio files. The attic's music box plays Brahms' Lullaby, and Lumi's guitar lessons teach Twinkle, Twinkle (both traditional, public domain).

              Code

              Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No libraries.
